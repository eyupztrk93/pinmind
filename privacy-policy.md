# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: July 13, 2026**

## Overview
PinMind ("we", "our", "the app") is a location-based reminder 
application. Your privacy is important to us. PinMind is designed 
to keep your data on your device: we do not operate any servers, 
and we never see your reminders or your location history.

## Data Collection
PinMind collects and uses the following data:

### Location Data
- **Purpose:** To trigger reminders when you arrive at or leave 
  specific locations, and to show your position on the map
- **"Always" permission:** PinMind asks for "Always" location 
  access so that iOS can deliver your reminders even when the app 
  is closed. Region monitoring is performed by iOS itself; the app 
  does not continuously track your position
- **Storage:** Your saved locations are stored **locally on your 
  device only**
- **Sharing:** We do **not** sell or share your location data with 
  anyone

### Reminder Data
- Reminder titles, notes, checklists, and associated locations are 
  stored **locally on your device**
- This data is **never** transmitted to external servers

## Third-Party Services
- **Google Maps Platform (Maps SDK for iOS, Places API, Maps 
  Static API):** Used to display maps, search for places, and 
  render the small map images shown in notifications. When a map 
  is displayed or a place search is performed, the relevant 
  coordinates and standard device information (such as device 
  identifiers and diagnostic data) are processed by Google to 
  provide the service, subject to the 
  [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). 
  See also 
  [Google Maps Platform privacy guidance](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/ios-sdk/privacy)
- **Apple StoreKit:** Used for the optional premium subscription. 
  Payments are processed entirely by Apple and are subject to 
  Apple's terms. We never see your payment details

## Data Storage
All your data is stored locally on your device using Apple's 
SwiftData framework. We do **not** operate any servers or 
cloud databases.

## Data Deletion
You can delete all your data at any time by:
- Deleting individual reminders and locations within the app
- Uninstalling the app from your device

## Children's Privacy
PinMind does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes 
will be reflected with an updated date at the top of this page.
